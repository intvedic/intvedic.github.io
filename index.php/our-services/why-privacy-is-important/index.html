WHY PRIVACY IS IMPORTANT

Do you collect, keep or process the personal data of the individuals (patients, employees, customers, children) like Name, Contact Number, Address, WhatsApp Number, Aadhaar, PAN, Images, Health Data (Diagnosis, Treatment, Prescription Letter, Medical History), Video recording, Bank Account Details?

Do you take and record the consent when collecting data?

Do you serve the privacy notice when collecting data?

Are you aware about the Indian law which mandates you to appoint a data protection officer to manage the requests of these individuals?

Do you share personal data with a third party? e.g. Business Partner, Service Provider, Vendors

IF YES, YOU MIGHT BE BREACHING THE PRIVACY REGULATION WHICH MAY AMOUNT TO A FINE UPTO 250 CRORE UNDER THE NEW DATA PRIVACY LAW OF INDIA.
